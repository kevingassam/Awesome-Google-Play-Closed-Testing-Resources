# Google Play Closed Testing Resources

A practical guide and resource list for Android developers who need testers for Google Play closed testing.

If you're preparing an Android app for Google Play production release, this repository collects useful resources, testing tips, and tools to help you organize your closed testing campaign.

### TestersTrade

[**TestersTrade**](https://www.testerstrade.com/) is a free tester exchange for Google Play closed testing. Developers join a Pack of up to 16 Android developers and test each other's apps for the full 14 days.

* Free, no payment
* A companion app checks every day that each app is still installed
* Drop-outs are replaced
* Testers leave structured feedback

👉 **Closed testing guide:** https://www.testerstrade.com/guide

## What is Google Play Closed Testing?

Google Play closed testing allows developers to distribute an app to a limited group of testers before making the application available to a wider audience.

Closed testing can help developers:

* Find bugs before release
* Collect feedback
* Test different Android devices
* Validate app functionality
* Prepare an app for production release

## How to Get Testers for Your Android App

Finding enough testers can be one of the most difficult parts of a closed testing campaign.

Here are some approaches:

1. Ask friends and colleagues
2. Share your testing link with Android communities
3. Contact developer communities
4. Use dedicated tester platforms
5. Build your own tester community
6. Ask testers to provide genuine feedback

## Google Play Testing Tips

Before starting your campaign:

* Make sure your application is installable
* Verify your Google Play testing configuration
* Prepare clear testing instructions
* Make sure testers know how to join the test
* Monitor tester participation
* Fix critical bugs discovered during testing
* Collect useful feedback from testers
